## FAQ section

**What's actually included in a package?**
Every package bundles content, design, and setup work that's normally sold separately — see the breakdown on each tier. Buying the bundle is always cheaper than adding the same pieces one at a time à la carte.

**How is this different from a $5k/month agency?**
No retainer, no monthly bill that keeps going after the work's done. You pay once for the package you pick, we deliver it, you own it.

**How long does delivery take?**
3–7 business days depending on the package — Starter Launch ships in 3–5 days, Growth Bundle and up in 5–7.

**What if I don't like what you deliver?**
You get one full round of revisions at no extra cost — just tell us what to adjust within 5 business days of delivery. Refunds aren't available once work has started, since real time goes into it right away, but between the revisions and how closely we work with you, a refund shouldn't be the thing you need.

**Do you only work with Shopify?**
Shopify's the main platform, but we also build on Wix and WordPress.

**Is the content actually AI-made, or does a real person do it?**
Both — AI handles the first pass so we can move fast and keep prices down, then a real person on the team reviews and finishes everything before it goes out. Nothing ships untouched.

**I don't know which package I need — what do I do?**
Tell us about your store and we'll recommend the right one, or start with an audit if you just want a second opinion first.

**Do I need to keep paying you after the site's live?**
No — maintenance plans exist if you want ongoing updates, backups, and support, but they're optional. Nothing renews automatically.
