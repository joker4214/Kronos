## 1. Lead magnet — "The Shopify Invisibility Checklist"

**Squeeze page copy** (two options only per the funnel spec: opt in or leave — no nav, no distractions):

> **Headline:** Your store looks amazing. So why isn't anyone buying?
>
> **Subhead:** The Shopify Invisibility Checklist — 10 things quietly killing your conversions, and exactly how to fix each one.
>
> ✅ Why your product photos aren't converting (it's not the photos)
> ✅ The one page every invisible store is missing
> ✅ A 5-minute GA4 check that tells you what's actually broken
> ✅ The SEO basics most store owners skip entirely
> ✅ How to tell if your ads are running or just burning money
>
> Free. Instant. Takes 10 minutes to run through your own store.
>
> **[ Get the checklist ]**
>
> No spam. One email with your checklist, then you're done — unless you want more.

**The 10 checklist items** (delivered as the actual PDF/page content):
1. **Homepage clarity** — can a stranger tell what you sell and why it matters in 5 seconds?
2. **Product photos** — are they telling a story, or just showing the product?
3. **Page speed** — is your store loading fast enough that people stick around?
4. **Mobile experience** — does checkout actually work cleanly on a phone?
5. **Analytics** — is GA4 (or anything) actually installed and tracking?
6. **SEO basics** — do your product titles and descriptions target real search terms?
7. **Social proof** — is there anything on the page proving other people bought and liked it?
8. **Ad-to-page match** — does what people click match what they land on?
9. **Email capture** — is there any way to keep a visitor who isn't ready to buy today?
10. **Consistent posting** — is there active content anywhere proving the brand is alive?

Each point ships with 2–3 sentences of plain-English "why it matters" + "quick fix," matching the Brand Guide's jargon-translation rule (e.g. "GA4 setup" always gets "you finally know what's working").

**Thank-you page copy** (right after opt-in — the excited moment, per the funnel spec):

> Checklist's on its way to your inbox.
>
> While you wait — want us to just do the first fix for you?
>
> **48-Hour Store Snapshot — $37**
> One branded photo placement + one optimized product listing, done for you, delivered in 48 hours.
>
> **[ Yes, snapshot my store — $37 ]**  or  **[ No thanks, just the checklist ]**
