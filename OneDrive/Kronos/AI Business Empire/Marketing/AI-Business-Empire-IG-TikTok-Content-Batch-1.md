## Option 1: The Quiz Hook (fastest to film, most direct)

**Format:** 15–20 sec, talking head or text-over-B-roll.

**On-screen hook (first 1–2 sec):**
> You don't know what AI business to build. I built something that tells you.

**Script / voiceover:**
> Everyone's telling you to "build something with AI." Nobody tells you WHAT.
>
> So I built a free 2-minute quiz. Five questions — what you're good at, what you actually do all day, what your instinct is when something's broken.
>
> By the end, it tells you exactly what to build — a course, a coaching offer, a tool, a service, or a community. One specific answer. Not a list.
>
> Link's in bio.

- "1 specific answer."
- "Free."

**Caption:**

## Option 2: The Framework Story (more personal, softer sell — rewritten 2026-08-09)

**Format:** 30–45 sec, talking head works best — this one needs to feel like you, not an ad.

**On-screen hook (first 1–2 sec):**
> I didn't build my AI assistant from scratch. That's exactly the point.

**Script / voiceover:**
> I run an AI assistant that manages my notes, my memory, basically runs my day-to-day ops across two businesses. I didn't invent it from zero — I started from an existing prompt framework someone else built, then customized it until it actually ran real work.
>
> That's the thing nobody tells you: you don't need to build something new from a blank page. You need one real starting point, and the willingness to actually customize it instead of just staring at it.
>
> Same idea behind this quiz. Answer 5 questions about what you're actually good at, and it tells you exactly what AI business fits you — a real starting point, not a blank page.
>
> Link's in bio if you want to find out.

**On-screen text overlays:**
- "I didn't build this from scratch."
- "I started from someone else's framework."
- "Then made it run two real businesses."

**Caption:**
> I didn't invent my AI assistant from zero — I started from an existing framework and built on it until it ran real work. Same idea behind this free quiz: a real starting point, not a blank page. Link in bio. 🔗
>
> #buildinpublic #AIbusiness #AItools #solopreneur #foundersjourney #AIautomation
