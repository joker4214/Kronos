## Day 1 — Problem-aware

### Ad 1A — Direct pain point
**Primary text:** You've got the AI skills, or you're building them. But there's a real gap between "I could build something with this" and "I have an actual business making money." Most people trying to cross that gap just guess.
**Headline:** You built an AI thing. Now what?
**CTA button:** Learn More

### Ad 1B — Relatable frustration
**Primary text:** Learned prompting. Watched the tutorials. Still don't have an actual AI business making money? You're not behind — you just never got a straight answer on what to build first.
**Headline:** Stop researching. Start building.
**CTA button:** Learn More

## Day 2 — Curiosity / quiz hook

**CTA button:** Take the Quiz

### Ad 2B — Contrarian hook
**Primary text:** Most "what AI business should I build" content gives you a list of 20 ideas and leaves you exactly where you started. This gives you one.

**CTA button:** Take the Quiz

## Notes for whoever runs these

- **No two consecutive days repeat the same angle** — pain point → curiosity → authority → specificity → urgency, so testing across the week actually tells you something about which hook works, not just ad fatigue.
- **Every CTA leads with the quiz, never a direct Gumroad pitch** — matches the funnel's own structure (quiz first, tripwire second) and jareds-takes' rule that ads are top-of-funnel awareness, not direct-response sales.
- **Before spending anything:** confirm Meta/TikTok pixel or GA4 conversion tracking is actually wired to `/start.html` and `/quiz.html` so ad performance is measurable — not confirmed as of this doc.
