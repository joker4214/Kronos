💡 Pro Tips for Success

Tip 1: One Line Per Day (At Minimum)

Don't try to finish all 10 lines in one week. Pick a rhythm that works for you. The key is consistency.

Tip 2: Be Honest in Your Worksheets

Don't write what sounds smart. Write what's TRUE. Honesty = better decisions.

Tip 3: Use Obsidian as Your Thinking Tool

Write messy. Write incomplete thoughts. That's okay. This is your THINKING TOOL, not a perfect document.

Tip 4: Review Weekly

Set 1 hour every Sunday to review what you filled out that week. Read your Obsidian notes. Add new thoughts. Link ideas. This is when it clicks.

Tip 5: Share Your Progress (Optional)

As you work through this, share your progress on Twitter or LinkedIn. Two benefits: people will want to buy what you build, and you'll stay accountable.

🆘 What If You Get Stuck?

Stuck on a Line?

Re-read the description. It's written in simple language. If still stuck: Skip it for now. Come back tomorrow with fresh eyes.

Don't Know How to Answer a Question?

Write what you THINK is the answer. You don't need certainty. You need to make a decision. Many answers will change as you learn. That's fine. That's progress.

Think You're Doing It Wrong?

You're not. There's no "wrong" way to fill out these worksheets. The only wrong way is not filling them out at all.
