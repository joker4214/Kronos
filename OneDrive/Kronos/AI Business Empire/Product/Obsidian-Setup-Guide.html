Obsidian Setup Guide

5-Minute Setup for Note-Taking

What is Obsidian?

Obsidian is a note-taking app where you'll save all your thinking about this business framework.

It's NOT like Notion or Google Docs. It's simpler. Faster. Built for thinking.

Think of it as your business thinking journal that's organized, searchable, and interconnected.

Why Use Obsidian?

  • ✅ Works offline — No internet needed
  • ✅ Your data is yours — Stored on your computer, not a company server
  • ✅ Free (forever) — No subscription required
  • ✅ Super fast — No lag, no loading
  • ✅ Built for thinking — Link ideas together, build knowledge

The 5-Minute Setup

1 Download Obsidian

Go to obsidian.md and click Download. It's free. Install it on your computer like any other app.

2 Create Your Vault

Open Obsidian. Click "Create new vault". Name it: My AI Business

A "vault" is just a folder on your computer where all your notes live.

3 Create 10 Folders

Inside your vault, create these 10 folders:

  • 01 - Foundation
  • 02 - Toolkit
  • 03 - Offers
  • 04 - Content
  • 05 - Leads
  • 06 - Sales
  • 07 - Delivery
  • 08 - Scaling
  • 09 - Monetization
  • 10 - Freedom
4 Create Notes for Each Line

In each folder, create a new note. Name it: Line X - [Name]

Examples:

  • Line 1 - Foundation
  • Line 2 - Toolkit
  • Line 3 - Offers
5 Add Your Worksheet Answers

Go back to the AI Business Empire Map. Fill out the worksheet for Line 1.

Then copy your answers into Obsidian's Line 1 note.

Repeat for all 10 lines.

Pro Feature: Linking Notes Together

This is where Obsidian gets powerful. You can link notes together.

When writing in a note, use double brackets: [[Line 1 - Foundation]]

This creates a clickable link. Later, you can see which notes link to each other.

Example: In your Line 3 (Offers) note, you might write:

This offer solves the problem described in [[Line 1 - Foundation]]

Now those two notes are connected. Click the link to jump between them.

Daily Workflow

Each day when you work through a line:

  1. Open the AI Business Empire Map
  2. Click on a line to expand it
  3. Fill out the worksheet questions
  4. Open Obsidian
  5. Go to that line's note
  6. Paste your answers
  7. Add extra thoughts or ideas
  8. Link to other notes if relevant
  9. Close Obsidian when done

Optional: Tagging & Organization

If you want to get fancy, use tags to organize your thoughts.

In Obsidian, type #idea, #decision, #problem, or #win anywhere in a note.

Examples:

  • #idea — New thoughts you might explore later
  • #decision — Decisions you've made
  • #problem — Challenges you need to solve
  • #win — Things you're proud of

Later, you can search for #idea and see all your ideas in one place.

Weekly Review (Very Important)

Set 1 hour every Sunday morning to review.

Open Obsidian. Read through all your notes from that week. Add new thoughts. Link ideas together. Fix things that don't make sense.

This is when everything clicks. This is where the real work happens.

FAQ

Do I HAVE to use Obsidian?

No. You can use Google Docs, Word, Notion, or a physical notebook. Obsidian is just recommended because it's free and works offline.

Will my notes be lost?

No. Your notes are stored on YOUR computer. They don't sync to the cloud (unless you set that up separately).

Is Obsidian hard to learn?

No. The basic setup takes 5 minutes. You'll figure out the rest as you go.

Can I change my mind about folder names?

Absolutely. Obsidian makes it easy to rename folders and notes.

What if I mess up?

You can't break anything. Just start over if needed.
