SAVE YOUR WORK -- READ THIS PART:
Your answers auto-save to whatever browser you're using, but that's not the
same as safe. Clear your browser's cache, switch to a different browser, or
open this on a new computer, and everything you typed is gone -- no warning,
no recovery. That's just how browser storage works, for any website.

The fix: click "💾 Save My Progress" any time you want a real backup. It
downloads a small file with all your answers. Move that file into the
"My Progress Saves" folder in this same package, so you always know where
it is. Next time you sit down to work -- even on a different computer --
open the main HTML file and click "📂 Load My Progress" to pick that file
and get everything back exactly where you left off.

Nothing is ever sent anywhere. The save file lives on your own computer,
in your own folder, and only goes wherever you put it.
