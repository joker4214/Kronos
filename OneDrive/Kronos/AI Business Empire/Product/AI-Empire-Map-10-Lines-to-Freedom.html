🗂️ Obsidian Setup Guide

What is Obsidian?

Obsidian is a note-taking app where you'll save all your thinking about this business framework.

It's simpler than Notion. Faster. Built for thinking.

Why Use Obsidian?

  • ✅ Works offline — No internet needed
  • ✅ Your data is yours — Stored on your computer
  • ✅ Free forever — No subscription required
  • ✅ Super fast — No lag
  • ✅ Built for thinking — Link ideas together

The 5-Minute Setup

Step 1: Download Obsidian

Go to obsidian.md and click Download. It's free. Install it like any other app.

Step 2: Create Your Vault

Open Obsidian. Click "Create new vault". Name it: My AI Business

Step 3: Create 10 Folders

Inside your vault, create these folders:

  • 01 - Foundation
  • 02 - Toolkit
  • 03 - Offers
  • 04 - Content
  • 05 - Leads
  • 06 - Sales
  • 07 - Delivery
  • 08 - Scaling
  • 09 - Monetization
  • 10 - Freedom

Step 4: Create Notes for Each Line

In each folder, create a new note. Name it: Line X - [Name]

Example: Line 1 - Foundation

Step 5: Add Your Worksheet Answers

Fill out a worksheet here, then copy your answers into Obsidian's corresponding note.

Pro Feature: Linking Notes

Use double brackets: [[Line 1 - Foundation]]

This creates clickable links between notes. Your ideas become connected.

Daily Workflow

  1. Open this map
  2. Click on a line to expand
  3. Fill out the worksheet
  4. Open Obsidian
  5. Paste your answers
  6. Add extra thoughts
  7. Link to other notes

Weekly Review (Very Important)

Set 1 hour every Sunday to review.

Open Obsidian. Read through all your notes from that week. Add new thoughts. Link ideas together. This is when everything clicks.
